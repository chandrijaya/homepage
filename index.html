Astronomy first

My degree is in astronomy from Institut Teknologi Bandung. The final project was a clustering problem: which stars in a patch of sky actually belong to a cluster, and which ones only look like they do.

I applied the minimum spanning tree algorithm to stellar proper motion data from the MWSC II catalog, in Python, across six open clusters. The method kept an average of 95.02% of high-probability member stars while cutting background objects to 22.45% of the original field.

Separating real records from noise is still most of what I do. The data changed; the problem did not.

Thesis

Determination of Open Cluster Members Using the Reduced Minimum Spanning Tree Method
